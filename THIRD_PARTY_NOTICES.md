# Third-party notices

The self-hosted browser viewers use the MIT-licensed projects listed below. Photo Sphere Viewer bundles its own Three.js 0.185.1; the compatibility renderer loads the separately vendored Three.js r157 from `frontend/vendor/three.min.js`. The PSV runtime assets and matching license copies are generated by `npm run build:vendor` and verified before either service starts. Generated PSV notices are also served at `/assets/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.

The shared UI icon sprite reuses Phosphor Regular SVG geometry under the MIT License. Source IDs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `frontend/icons/manifest.json`; the license is included in [frontend/icons/LICENSE.phosphor](frontend/icons/LICENSE.phosphor) and served at `/assets/icons/LICENSE.phosphor`.
